Reward anticipation in adolescents: an example paper

Abstract
This is a made-up paper used as a contract example. Twenty participants performed a monetary incentive delay task.

Methods
Images were thresholded at p < 0.001 uncorrected, with a cluster extent of 20 voxels, and clusters were family-wise error corrected at p < 0.05. Coordinates are reported in MNI space.

Results
Table 1 lists the peaks for Gain > Neutral. Loss > Neutral produced no significant activation.
